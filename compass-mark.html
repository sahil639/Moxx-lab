Lobes

Lobes are equal circles set around a ring. Spread pushes every other lobe in or out. The fillet is a circle rolled into each notch between two neighbours.

Spiral channel

Each channel is the sliver between a big circle and a slightly smaller one. Offsetting the smaller one makes the cut taper like a blade.
